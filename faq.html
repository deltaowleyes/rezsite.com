Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions.

Websites, free addresses, pricing and the directory, answered in plain words.

What is RezSite?

RezSite designs websites for Indigenous businesses and runs a free, searchable directory of Indigenous businesses, churches and community events across Canada. Our websites are built with BAKE.

What's free?

Every site we build gets a free yourname.rezsite.com address, free hosting, and a free preview before you pay anything.

Is there a catch with free hosting?

Just one: a small “Baked with BAKE” badge sits in your website's footer.

How much does a custom design cost?

Custom design starts at $100, one time. We quote the price before any work you pay for begins.

How long until I see my preview?

Usually 1–2 days. We design it first and send you a link to look over on your phone. No payment, no pressure.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We'll give it a fresh new look the same way: free preview first, then a quote.

How do I get started?

Type your business name in the box on the home page, or email us. Tell us about your business, your photos, your logo and the one thing your website must do: calls, bookings or orders.

Is my business in the directory?

Search the directory by name or town. If your business is missing or a detail is wrong, email us and we'll fix it.

What does “Verified” mean?

A verified listing was confirmed by the business owner. Verified businesses can add their logo for free. See the verified businesses.

What does “Unverified” mean?

Most listings come from public-source research done for Delta Owl Eyes. “Unverified” means the business hasn't confirmed the details with us yet, so please contact the business before relying on them.

Who will I be talking to?

Real people. When you email, you'll hear back from Chris or Leah, not a robot.
