Questions

Good to know.

Anything else? Email chris@rezsite.com.

Are the three designs really free?

Yes. They cost nothing and you’re under no obligation. If none of them is right for you, just say no thanks.

What does it cost if I like one?

A one-time price, usually $180 to $400 depending on the design. There are no monthly fees. For a bigger site, we quote it after you’ve seen the designs.

Where will my website live?

Hosting is free at an address like yourbusiness.rezsite.com, with a small “Baked with BAKE” badge at the bottom of your pages. To remove the badge, there’s a small monthly fee based on how busy your site is, starting at $5 a month.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. We connect a domain you already own for $100, one time. Or we register a new one for you for $50, plus the yearly domain fee (usually about $15 a year).

What do I need to send you?

Nothing to start. We use the words, photos and logo from your current website or listing. You can send new photos or changes any time.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer. We send the details along with your designs.

My business is in the directory. Can I change or remove the listing?

Yes. Email us and we’ll correct your listing or remove it, whichever you prefer.
