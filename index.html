Case study · a marina on the lake

What we build, piece by piece.

Pinecrest Bay Marina (a sample business) needed boaters to see slip rates and book fast — on a phone, on the water. Here's every part we built, and why.

  1. A hero that's really theirsTheir own aerial photo of the docks, the lake's coordinates and one job up top: Book a slip.
  2. The numbers boaters ask for180 slips, 6 ft depth, VHF channel 68 and the anchor rating — answered before anyone calls.
  3. Slip rates, in a real tableSeasonal and transient prices by boat length. No PDF, no “call for pricing.”
  4. Fuel dock and boat storageCovered slips, open slips, the fuel dock and storage — each easy to find, none buried in a menu.
  5. Built for a thumb on a boat“Book a slip” and “See slip rates” as big buttons on the phone, right under the headline.
