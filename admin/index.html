Call script

Offer: 3 free custom designs within 1–2 business days. No cost, no obligation.

1. Opening

“Hi, this is [your name] calling from BAKE, a web design studio. Could I speak with the owner or whoever looks after your website?”
“Thanks! I’ll be quick. We came across [business] and took a look at your website. We’d like to make you three custom designs for a new site, free, built around your business, your photos and your logo. You’d have them in your inbox within one or two business days, and there’s no obligation at all. Would you like us to make them for you?”

2. If yes, collect

  1. Contact person (name + role)
  2. Email (read it back letter by letter)
  3. Logo: “Could you email us your logo? Any size is fine.”
  4. Photos: “Photos of your work, team, food or place? Bigger is better.”
  5. Pages: Home, About, Services, Menu, Gallery, Contact…
“Perfect. You’ll get three designs from us by [day]. If you like one, we’ll walk you through the next steps then. Thanks!”

3. Voicemail (under 20 seconds)

“Hi, this is [name] from BAKE, a web design studio. We’d like to make [business] three free custom website designs, no obligation. I’ll try you again soon. Have a great day.”

Objections

We already have a website.That’s exactly why we called. We’d show you what a fresh version could look like. If you like yours better, nothing changes. It’s free.
How much?The designs are free. If you like one, simple sites start at about $100; bigger custom sites are quoted after you’ve seen the designs.
What’s the catch?No catch. We make the designs, you look at them. If you don’t love one, you just say no thanks.
I’m busy.It takes two minutes: I just need an email and what pages you’d want. Or when’s a better time? (→ Call back later)
Send me information.The best information is the designs themselves, made for you. What email should they go to?
Someone does our website.Great, they can have a look too. Three new designs cost nothing and might give you both ideas.
Need to ask my partner / board.Of course. The designs make that easy. Can I send them to you so you can share them?
How did you get my number?We’re BAKE, a small web design studio. Your business is in our directory, and your number is listed publicly with your website.
Not interested.No problem, thanks for your time. (Don’t push.)
Don’t call again.Understood, I’ll take you off our list. (→ Do not call again)
Can’t answer it?Good question. I’ll have Leah get back to you on that. (Write it in Next step.)

Never

Promise prices, dates or features beyond the above · say a business is Indigenous-owned unless they tell you · argue.
